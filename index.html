How to Play

CONTROLS

Desktop: Arrow keys or W A S D to steer. Space or Escape pauses.
Mobile: Swipe to steer. Tap to pause. One turn is always queued, so swipe early.

SURVIVAL

Energy nodes extend your trail permanently. The board wraps at every edge — leave one side and you appear on the other. Hitting your own trail, a sparking component or an energised barrier ends the run, unless a Shield absorbs it.

SCORING

100 points per node, plus a bonus that grows with your speed tier. Collect nodes quickly to build a combo multiplier up to 4×.

POWER-UPS

Slow Motion eases the current · Double Points doubles scoring · Magnet pulls in nearby nodes · Shield absorbs one fatal hit.

CAMPAIGN

30 levels across six environments. Objectives include collecting a quota, activating terminals in order, linking terminal pairs, and charging an exit before the timer runs long. Earn up to three stars per level.
